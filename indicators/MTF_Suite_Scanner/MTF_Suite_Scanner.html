MTF Suite — Scanner



Description:

The MTF Suite: Scanner is an advanced multi-timeframe technical indicator suite designed for traders seeking a comprehensive market overview. It aggregates signals from multiple indicators across different timeframes to generate precise buy and sell alerts. The script focuses on flexibility, allowing traders to customize alerts, indicator parameters, and watchlists to suit various trading styles.

Key Features:

- Supports RSI, MFI, MACD, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, TSI, ADX, Supertrend, and up to 5 Moving Averages.

- Fully configurable alert types: Buy, Sell, or Both.

- Customizable Moving Average types including EMA, SMA, DEMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, LSMA, ALMA, FRAMA, KAMA, and others.

- Multi-symbol watchlist with up to 40 symbols for scanning.

- Table display with multiple sizing and positioning options.

- Adjustable indicator thresholds for overbought/oversold conditions.

- Supertrend and ADX filtering for trend confirmation.

- Multi-timeframe support for all indicators.

How It Works:

The script scans selected symbols across multiple timeframes and applies technical indicators to each. It compares indicator readings to user-defined thresholds to determine potential buy or sell conditions. Signals are displayed in an easy-to-read table with visual highlights for strong trends or alerts. The multi-timeframe analysis allows traders to see the broader trend and identify optimal entry/exit points.

Customization:

- Change the Moving Average type and length for each MA line.

- Set custom thresholds for indicators like RSI, MFI, or Stochastic to adjust sensitivity.

- Enable or disable specific indicators or alerts as needed.

- Resize and reposition the table to fit your chart layout.

- Adjust Supertrend, ADX, or other filter parameters to match your trading style.

- Configure alerts for buy, sell, or both to receive notifications based on your preferences.

Intended Use:

This script is intended for serious traders looking to streamline market analysis by consolidating key technical indicators into a single, easily readable dashboard. It works best for forex, indices, commodities, and selected stocks.
